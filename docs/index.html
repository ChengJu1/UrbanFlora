What makes it different

A handful of small ideas, sweated over.

✦

Sensor-native capture

Camera, GPS, compass and the current weather all get woven into every observation — no manual fiddling.

◎

ML you can correct

Pl@ntNet gives you three candidates with confidence rings; you always pick the right one. No silent mistakes.

❀

A codex, not a feed

Finds are grouped by botanical family. Each family chapter has locked tiles that pull you back out on another walk.

↻

Streaks that mean something

A custom-painted streak ring rewards daily noticing. Miss a day and it quietly resets; no guilt, just a nudge.

✉

Daily digest

Once a day, a calm modal summarises your species, families and rarest find — mini-stories, never push spam.

▲

Cloud-synced, yours

Firebase Auth and Firestore keep your codex across devices. Your observations, your data, exportable later.

How it works

Four steps, every day.

1. Notice

Onboarding trains the habit of looking down.

2. Identify

Pl@ntNet returns top-3 species in seconds.

3. Collect

Your codex grows; so does the streak ring.

4. Reflect

Evening digest tells you the shape of your day.
