# UrbanFlora Storyboard

A day in the life of **Li Wei**, our curious-student persona.

## Frame 1 — Morning, 08:42, Bloomsbury

Li Wei waits for the crossing on Gower Street. A small purple flower is pushing up between the paving stones. They open UrbanFlora and the splash leaf blooms.

> *Beat: the splash screen is already bright; it invites action, not a "please wait".*

## Frame 2 — Home dashboard

The streak ring reads **4 days**. A soft "You are a sensor node" tagline sits beside it. Li Wei feels the pull of not breaking the streak.

> *Beat: a streak is the simplest pattern that brings people back the next day.*

## Frame 3 — The shutter

A single big green FAB *Spot a plant*. Tap. The camera opens with the compass overlay showing the device is pointed South-East.

## Frame 4 — The capture

Shutter depresses with a satisfying spring animation. The photo file is picked up by the Pl@ntNet service while Li Wei keeps walking.

## Frame 5 — Identification

Three candidate cards slide in, top to bottom. A 0.84 confidence ring highlights the first: *Bellis perennis, "Common daisy"*. Li Wei glances, taps the card, and adds a note: "outside NPL."

## Frame 6 — Reward

A rarity badge flips in: *Common*. The Save button turns into a check. The codex streak ring will bump to **5 days** on return.

## Frame 7 — Detail page

A SliverAppBar unfurls the photo, then metadata: captured at 08:44, Bloomsbury WC1, facing 135° (SE), 14.2°C clear sky. Marcus-the-data-journalist-persona will later export this row.

## Frame 8 — Evening

Li Wei opens the app at 21:30. A gentle modal summarises the day: *3 species, 1.4 km travelled, 0 legendary finds.* The codex page for *Asteraceae* has two of six squares filled in.
